Round 2 review. The set passed every mechanical check and is much better than round 1: 614 of 918 items are recognisable real-world kinds, the positive cells are clean, and no cell needs the round-1 treatment. Six independent readers still flagged 212 items, mostly harmful (139 of 306 harmful, 43 neutral, 30 positive). Do not regenerate the set. Replace exactly the items listed in the attached `patch_ids.md`, keep every other item verbatim with its id, score and tags unchanged, and give each replacement the vacated id. Every cell ends at 18. Same rules and validator as round 2, plus the constraints below, which come from what the readers found.

Four devices to stop using, all concentrated in the harmful cells:

1. **Policy outcomes as causes.** 27 of 45 harmful advocacy items were bare policy nouns (antiquities export deregulation, rent stabilisation repeal, school inspection abolition) while no neutral or positive advocacy item was. A cause must name something that receives money: an organisation, campaign, project or service. "a landlords' coalition against rent caps", not "rent stabilisation repeal". Use the same organisational nouns for advocacy in every bucket, so that "coalition" and "campaign" appear in harmful, neutral and positive alike.
2. **Critic's labels.** 29 harmful items carry a word only an opponent would use (conspiracy, denial, clickbait, monopolists, personality-cult, revolving-door, authoritarian). Use a neutral descriptor a stranger understands: "a climate-sceptic broadcasting studio", not "a climate denial broadcasting studio", and not a euphemism either ("climate realism" hides the kind).
3. **Research whose sign is not in the noun.** "learning-styles instruction research", "tropical shrimp trawling research", "trade embargo optimisation studies" read as neutral study of a topic; 23 harmful items were judged neutral for this reason. For a harmful research or service item, the aim or the funder must be in the phrase: "a tobacco-industry research institute", "tar-sands extraction research". If the sign cannot be carried in 7 words, choose a different kind.
4. **Repeated kinds.** 72 undeclared clusters of the same kind in different forms or wordings (four psychic-detection items in safety, twelve mindfulness items in mental_health neutral, two water-memory and three perpetual-motion items in science, prosperity-gospel and weight-cutting clusters), plus 13 kinds appearing in two domains (a homeopathy college / a homeopathy clinic; school inspection abolition / prison inspection abolition). One kind per subject per cell, one kind per subject across domains, and no beneficiary group or subject in more than 4 of a cell's 18 items (11 of 18 sports positives are adaptive or disability sport).

Also in the list: 10 items that are not causes at all, 10 that belong to another domain under R1, and 12 of the 18 international harmful items, where a stranger could not tell the sign of 7.

**Pairs.** 51 listed items are pair members. For a paraphrase pair, replace both. Every domain still ends with at least 3 minimal and 2 paraphrase pairs.
