# Cause domains

A fixed set of 17 domains for classifying causes that people might donate to. The domains partition the space: every cause belongs to exactly one domain. A domain is set by what the funded activity _does_, not by who benefits, where it happens, or who runs it. Where scope lists overlap, the tie-break rules at the end decide, and they take precedence over the lists.

## Tie-break rules

- **R1 Advocacy sits with its subject.** A lobby about energy policy is `environment`, a vaccine campaign on either side is `physical_health`, a firearms group is `safety`. `politics` is reserved for the machinery of government and democracy itself: elections, parties, administration, national security, immigration.
- **R2 Geography does not set the domain.** Bednets in a malaria zone are `physical_health`; a village school is `education`; a well is `environment` or `physical_health` by its purpose. `international` is for causes whose object is the international system: peace, diplomacy, trade, exchange.
- **R3 Individual service versus system.** Services delivered to people in need are `social`; market-, infrastructure- or policy-level work is `economy`; legal representation and rights litigation are `justice`.
- **R4 Food and land: produce, protect, distribute.** Growing food and running supply systems is `agriculture`; conserving land, water and forests is `environment`; giving food or shelter to people in need is `social`.
- **R5 Species versus individuals.** Conserving a species or habitat is `environment`; the welfare of individual animals, wild or domestic, is `animals`.
- **R6 Research and education follow the subject.** Research on human disease is `physical_health` or `mental_health`; other research is `science`. Formal schooling and degrees, including professional schools, are `education`; outreach and informal education about a subject go with that subject.
- **R7 Who runs it does not set the domain.** A soup kitchen run by a church is `social`; a hospital run by a religious order is `physical_health`. `religion` is for worship, ministry, missions, religious buildings and religious training.
- **R8 Body versus mind.** Conditions of the mind and substance use are `mental_health`; neurological and all other disease is `physical_health`; ongoing living support for disabled people, whatever the cause of disability, is `social`.
- **R9 Beneficiary group does not set the domain.** Causes for children, women, veterans, refugees, the elderly or the wealthy are classified by the activity: youth football is `sports`, youth counselling is `mental_health`, youth foster care is `social`, women's rights litigation is `justice`, women's health clinics are `physical_health`.
