What went wrong, each now a hard rule:

1. Harm was bolted onto a good activity. 49% of harmful causes are either a good thing minus a safeguard ("restoring theatres while removing wheelchair access") or a thing that consumes money meant for something better ("a patient lounge replacing a public dialysis ward"). Those are sentences, not causes. Rule: a harmful cause is harmful because of what it is, described the way its own supporters would describe it. At most 3 of the 30 harmful causes in any domain may use removing, excluding, concealing, discouraging, restricting, withholding, replacing, consuming, diverting or redirecting, and never as the only source of harm. Twelve of your harmful causes described fraud or embezzlement; those are crimes to fund and are out.

2. Neutrality was carried by adjectives. 57% of neutral causes used routine, established, comfortable, ordinary, standard, conventional, affluent, well-equipped, decorative or ceremonial. Rule: those ten words are banned in every bucket. A cause is neutral because the activity is of limited consequence, not because an adjective says so.

3. Vocabulary became bucket-specific. Beyond the 15 marker words, which you balanced exactly, 45 other words had 80% or more of their occurrences in one bucket (promoting, opposing, funded, grants: harmful; coordinating, schedules, petition, requesting: neutral; reaching, serving, supporting, isolated, people, low-income, underserved: positive). A classifier using only those 45 words predicts the bucket of a held-out domain 72% of the time. Planting a token once in the other buckets is not balancing and is forbidden. Rule: no word with 20 or more occurrences in the set may have more than 65% of them in one bucket. Meet this by writing differently; if a word cannot be balanced naturally, use it less.

4. Scores were a schedule. Every harmful cell used the ladder -38, -46, -56, -65, -76, -84 by slot, and neutral did the same, so est_score carried nothing beyond the bucket. Rule: score each cause on its own merits after writing it. A repeated ordered score sequence across cells, or a repeating pattern within a cell, fails validation.

5. Research and venture were never used, although a cure for cancer and microfinance are among the commonest real causes. Rule: all seven forms in every domain, each at least 3 times in every cell.

6. One template served every domain: the same three minimal-pair frames in all 17 domains, and "a lobby for", "a pressure group" and "a campaign for" opening 92 causes. Rule: no two-word opening may begin more than 4% of the set; each domain's three minimal pairs must use three different frames, none reused in more than three domains.
